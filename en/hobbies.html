Reading & Writing

Literary studies

Studying Chinese language and literature; my undergraduate thesis is on narrative art in Philip K. Dick's Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? The hardest part of sci-fi turned out not to be the premise — it's how people get consumed by their own premise.

Narratology Sci-fi

Long-form & Eastern fantasy

I read serialized web novels, and I like how tightly Eastern fantasy builds its worlds. What I love is the image of one person becoming an army — not by cheats, but by grinding insight into the body.

19 columns on Zhihu, a running WeChat publication. Writing isn't an output channel for me — it's how vague things get forced into a single sentence. If I can't say it, I haven't thought it.
